Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Muhammad Shahid Aslam, an independent app developer ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information in Word Fuse, Hue Dive, and this website.

Information We Collect

Word Fuse and Hue Dive do not require an account. We do not collect your name or email address through the apps. The apps and their enabled advertising, analytics, and crash-reporting services may collect or receive an advertising identifier (GAID on Android or IDFA on iOS), device and app information, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, usage and diagnostic events, and crash or performance data.

This static website does not use contact forms or tracking scripts. Its hosting provider may process IP addresses and standard request information in server logs to deliver and protect the site. If you email us, we will receive the address and information you choose to include.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate and improve the apps, understand app performance and usage, diagnose crashes, protect against fraud or abuse, measure advertising, limit ad frequency, and deliver or personalize ads where permitted. Approximate location may be used by advertising partners for regional ad selection and measurement; we do not use it to identify your precise location.

Advertising and Third-Party SDKs

The apps use InMobi advertising services and may also use Google AdMob. These providers may process advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction data under their own privacy practices. Review the InMobi Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy. Other platform providers, including Apple and Google Play, may process information when you download or use the apps. The apps do not use accounts or social login.

Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can limit or reset your advertising identifier and opt out of personalized advertising in your device's privacy or advertising settings. The exact controls depend on your device and operating system. Where required, we or our advertising partners will obtain consent, including consent required under the GDPR, or honor opt-out rights and signals, including those applicable under the CCPA, before processing data for personalized advertising or other regulated purposes. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, delete, correct, or restrict certain personal information, or to opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. You can contact us to make a request; we may need information to verify it.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including app operation, analytics, security, and legal obligations. Retention periods vary by data type and service provider. Advertising, analytics, and crash-reporting providers handle their own retention according to their policies and settings.

Children's Privacy

Word Fuse and Hue Dive are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through an app, contact us so we can take appropriate steps. The apps are not designed to knowingly serve personalized advertising to children.

Security
